RANDOM
CAR LORE.

Why do supercars have spoilers?

They can generate aerodynamic downforce, helping the tyres stay loaded at speed. The exact design is always a compromise between grip and drag.

What is a flat-plane crank?

A crankshaft arrangement used in some V8s where the crankpins are spaced differently from a cross-plane V8. It can enable high-revving character and strong exhaust pulse separation.

Why are tyres everything?

Because the tyres are the tiny contact patches actually connecting the car to the road. Power, brakes and aero ultimately have to get through them.

What is torque?

Rotational force. Engine torque is usually measured in N·m, and gearing multiplies it before it reaches the wheels.

Why do engines rev?

RPM tells you how quickly the crankshaft is rotating. More RPM means more combustion cycles per minute, but friction and mechanical stress rise too.

What makes AWD different?

All-wheel drive can send torque to all four wheels, potentially improving traction during acceleration and in low-grip conditions.
